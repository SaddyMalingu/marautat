What the fee covers

  • WhatsApp sales line — answers from your own catalogue, in your voice, and hands an order to a human when it should
  • M-Pesa collection — a prompt on the buyer's phone, a receipt per order, reconciled against your merchant code
  • Tenant portal — catalogue, orders, customers, abandoned carts, broadcasts; one sign-in link, no password to copy
  • Writer's Flow — describe an offer, get the pages that rank for it and a research queue behind them
  • The agent lane (Work_Anda) — applications filled for paid AI-task and chatter work; a human always presses submit

What it does not cover

  • Guaranteed sales, hires or income — nobody can sell that, and we will not pretend to
  • Custom development beyond the published scope
  • Third-party costs: ad spend, domains, Meta and Daraja fees

Why the fee is worth paying

The same platform has earned USD 566 in Mercor referral commission to date — our own ledger, not a projection. The engine wrote the pages, put the apply button where a person actually presses it, and the commission followed. That figure is what the machine did for us; it is not a forecast for you.

A month is only worth paying for once the catalogue is loaded and an order has gone through end to end. Until then we do not invoice — an empty shop is not a product.
