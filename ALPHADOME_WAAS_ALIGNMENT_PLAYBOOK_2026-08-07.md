## 5) How To Increase Odds Of Landing These Opportunities

## Positioning moves (high impact)
1. Show reliability artifacts, not just feature demos.
- For every outreach target, include: failure cases, eval criteria, retry/rollback story.

2. Speak in their language.
- Use their exact problem terms: GEO action loops, branch lifecycle, telephony deliverability, scheduling social constraints, etc.

3. Lead with 7-day shippable proof.
- Offer one concrete proof asset per target, with success metric and handoff path.

4. Make customer-facing execution visible.
- Highlight evidence that Alphadome builds with real users and real payments, not sandbox-only prototypes.

## Suggested readiness KPI before outreach burst
- 14/17 brand assets have one-pager + clickable demo/video + architecture note.
- At least 5 assets include measurable eval outputs (before/after quality metric or failure-reduction metric).
- 100% of target messages reference one specific workflow pain from the target’s own hiring page.
