- Deeply understand client's business, operations, and goals
- Identify the right 3.0 system for their needs
- Qualify Alpha 1 Squad fit
- Build strong founder relationship

- Initial 3.0 system recommendation

**Deliverables:**
- Discovery call notes and recording
- Business process documentation
- Opportunity analysis (prioritized)
- Preliminary 3.0 system recommendation
- Next steps confirmation

**Success Criteria:**
- Client understands Alphadome approach and value prop
- Agreement on scope and 3.0 system direction
- Buy-in from client leadership
- Clear understanding of timeline and investment

**Client Involvement:**
- Attend discovery call

### Phase 4: Avatar Design & Prototyping (Weeks 9-14, parallel to implementation)

**Objectives:**
- Finalize Avatar design
- Create prototype
- Plan physical manufacturing
- Test avatar behavior with AI agents
- Prepare for manufacturing and deployment

**Activities:**
- Design workshops (2-3 sessions)
  - Review 8 production Avatar designs
  - Collect client feedback on aesthetics
  - Iterate on customizations (color, features, personality)
  - Create digital prototype(s)

- Avatar personality & LLM fine-tuning
  - Define avatar "personality" (tone, style, values)
  - Fine-tune LLM to match avatar voice
  - Test conversational flows
  - Refine based on feedback

- Manufacturing planning
  - Partner with robotics/manufacturing firm
  - Create bill of materials (BOM)
  - Define manufacturing timeline
  - Plan quality assurance and testing
  - Estimate unit cost and royalty structure

- Physical prototype testing
  - Build working prototype
  - Test in simulated customer environment
  - Refine mechanics, sensors, interaction model
  - Client validation and feedback

**Deliverables:**
- Avatar design renderings (final approved)
- Avatar personality and LLM fine-tuning document
- Manufacturing partner agreement
- Bill of materials and cost estimate
- Manufacturing timeline (weeks to delivery)
- Physical prototype (if available)
- Avatar deployment plan (where and how to use)
- Avatar user manual and training

**Success Criteria:**
- Avatar design approved by client
- Personality/LLM tested and working
- Manufacturing partner identified and contracted
- Timeline and cost estimates confirmed
- Client confident in robot design and deployment

**Client Involvement:**
- Attend design workshops
- Provide feedback on iterations
- Approve final design
- Confirm manufacturing timeline
- Plan deployment location and use cases

**Alphadome Team:**
- Avatar Designer (CAD, visual design)
- AI Engineer (LLM fine-tuning)
- Manufacturing Manager (partners, timelines, costs)
- Mechanical Engineer (if prototype required)
- Success Manager (client coordination)

## PART 6: SUPPORT & ESCALATION

### Support Tiers

**Tier 1: Community Support (Founder's Package)**
- **Channel:** Slack community, email, FAQ
- **Response Time:** 24-48 hours
- **Included:** General questions, how-to guides, community help
- **Excluded:** Urgent issues, custom development, priority support

**Tier 2: Standard Support (Alpha 1 Squad)**
- **Channel:** Dedicated Slack channel, email, scheduled calls
- **Response Time:** 4-8 hours (business hours), 24 hours (off-hours)
- **Included:** Priority response, dedicated support contact, custom guidance
- **Excluded:** System development, 24/7 support, SLA guarantees

**Tier 3: Premium Support (Enterprise)**
- **Channel:** Dedicated Slack bot, WhatsApp, phone hotline
- **Response Time:** 1 hour (any time, including nights/weekends)
- **Included:** 24/7 support, dedicated engineer, priority bug fixes, SLA guarantees
- **Excluded:** Feature development (use product roadmap)
