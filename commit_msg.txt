Social listening: the intent taxonomy finally has eyes

The intents, phrases and scorer have been in this repo since the ABOSS work and
not one of them ever saw a post, because nothing produced
data/ABOSS_SOCIAL_SIGNALS_RAW.csv. The tenant's own signed-in browser is the only
actor that can close that gap without an account of ours, so the listener reads
LinkedIn content search and the portal does the judging.

- migrations/2026-11-04_social_signals.sql: the table (one row per tenant+post),
  plus the run-kind widening for 'listen'. Nothing touches opportunities.
- utils/socialIntent.js: the scoring lifted out of the ops script and made pure --
  no fabrication (a post matching no intent is dropped, not scored low),
  exclusions beat triggers, Kenya-first phone normalisation, and a draft first
  line that quotes the post instead of inventing an offer.
- utils/browserActor/tasks/linkedinListen.js: the DOM expressions, the last-week
  search URL, and a jittered 4.5-11s pace. Reads only; nothing can click or send.
- scripts/browser_actor_linkedin_listen.mjs: asks the portal which searches to
  run, opens them in the tenant's profile, posts raw text in chunks of 15.
- routes/agentRuns.js: POST /runs/:id/signals scores server-side (a worker cannot
  rank a tenant's prospects), counts what was excluded and off-topic, and leaves
  the run open for the next batch. /context sends the searches down.
- routes/writerFlow.js + panel: a Listening card with the digest, "copy first
  line", "I sent it" / "Not relevant" -- and no way to message anyone.
- Worker dispatches by platform from a map now, and refuses a platform it has no
  runner for instead of defaulting to Micro1.

Two silent failures found while building, both recorded in OPEN_FLAGS #39: the
agent routes read run.platform while the queue writes detail.platform (a listen
run resolved as micro1 and would have driven the wrong site), and scorePost read
post.text while the worker shape is postText (the first scored batch was empty).
