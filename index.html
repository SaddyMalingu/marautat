A content engine that writes like your business

Most "AI blog" tools produce nine variations of the same outline. Ours writes a nine-page journey per offer, in a voice assigned to your business, then checks its own work before anything is published.

One offer, nine pages

Every offer gets a full journey: what it is, what it costs, whether it fits, how to prepare, how to start, how to scale, and how to get value in year one. Each page owns its own URL, its own search intent and its own call to action — no duplicate copy across the set.

Your voice, not the model's

Six editorial voices — warm local expert, precise operator, bold challenger, measured advisor, storytelling guide, practical teacher — are matched to each tenant and kept stable across runs. Set a tone in your settings and it wins. The brief names your reader, your market and the scene to open on.

Measured before it publishes

A quality gate scores every draft: prose versus bullet points, paragraph depth, headings, specific numbers, an honest limitations section and a working call to action. Pages that read as an outline get rewritten automatically. The tenant portal shows the score instead of a bare "done".

Questions buyers actually ask

What can be live in the first 30 days?

One workflow. The fastest to deploy are WhatsApp campaign and follow-up flows, M-Pesa scan-to-pay checkout for a product catalogue, and the content engine for an existing offer. Each depends on integrations you already have — we confirm that in discovery before quoting, not after.

Do I have to replace my current tools?

No. We connect to what you already use where an integration is realistic, and we tell you plainly when something should be replaced instead of wrapped. The deployment is judged on the workflow working, not on how much of your stack we replaced.

Is this just ChatGPT with a dashboard?

No. A language model is used where it is genuinely the right tool — drafting, classification, summarising a conversation. Everything that touches money, stock, permissions or customer records runs through deterministic code with audit trails, fallbacks and role checks around it.

How is the content engine different from other AI blog tools?

Three differences you can verify: it writes a nine-page journey per offer instead of nine variations of one page; each tenant gets a stable editorial voice rather than a generic one; and every draft is scored against a quality gate (prose versus bullets, depth, specifics, an honest limitation section, a call to action) before it publishes.

Can my agency resell it?

Yes, through a written licence starting from $10,000. Tenant limits, hosting, support, updates, permitted reselling and any revenue share are defined in that agreement. We discuss fit before terms, so a licence is not sold to an agency that cannot support it.

What happens if the pilot does not work?

Acceptance criteria are agreed before the build starts, and there is a review checkpoint at the end of the pilot. If the workflow does not meet the agreed measures, we say so and either fix the scope or stop — with the evidence in front of both sides. We do not publish results we cannot stand behind.

Do you have client case studies?

Not yet published with evidence and permission. The systems on this site are shown as demos and build walkthroughs, labelled as such. When a deployment has a baseline, a measurement period and written permission, it will appear here as a case study — not before.

Give us the bottleneck, the tools you already use and your timeline. We will come back with a scoped recommendation: what to deploy first, what it will take, and what we would measure.
