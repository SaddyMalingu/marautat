**Written:** 2026-11-19 · **Why:** `docs/HASHLIST_EVALUATION.md` §6 — our cards are worth
low hundreds of dollars and must be spent carefully; introducing an owner of real
engineering records is the only route here with a five-figure ceiling.

## What Hashlist actually buys

Their data-set form's own categories, in the words of the seller:

> **Industrial and robotics** — programmable logic controller code · commissioning punch
> lists · robot field incident logs
> **Quality and safety** — 8D and warranty reports · non-conformance and corrective
> actions · safety analyses · audit findings · engineering change orders
> **Test and measurement** — test campaigns and reports · measurement and sensor logs ·
> hardware-in-the-loop scripts · vehicle bus traces · calibration data

And their rule for who may sell it: *"My company owns it and I can sign"* — or, the option
that matters to us, **"I can introduce the owner"**.

**So the product is not "data", it is records a firm already generates while doing
engineering work, and the seller must be the firm.** Which means the asset we are short of
is not prose or tasks. It is one relationship with an operations or engineering director.

## The ask — one message, no deck

To an operations or engineering director we already know:

```
Quick question, not a sale.

A Finnish company called Hashlist buys real engineering records — commissioning punch
lists, 8D and warranty reports, PLC code, calibration data, sensor logs — and licenses
them to AI labs. They pay $500 to $5,000 for a sample of about 1% and then license the
rest in batches, with a data licence your lawyers would sign.

If your team keeps any of that, would you take a 20-minute call? I'd bring their
categories and an NDA, and you'd decide whether it is interesting. If it is, you sell
the data and I take a share of the introduction.
```

Then a discovery set of six questions, in this order: which records exist, how far back,
who owns them, what is personal data inside them, what a redaction pass would cost, and who
signs.

The realistic 90-day outcome is **$0**, and that is why this stays a background action
(three messages a week) rather than a project. It is the only line here that can pay four
figures, so it is worth the three messages even at low odds.
